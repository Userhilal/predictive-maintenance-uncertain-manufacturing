## Conclusions we can support now

- The original downloaded files were successfully processed by the existing raw-data auditor.
- Published population/readout counts match the measured output.
- Vehicle identifiers were reported disjoint across splits, and the auditor reported explicit vehicle-to-target linkage.
- No malformed sensor numbers or adjacent backward/repeated timestamps were reported.
- Missing sensor values are present, though no feature exceeds 1% missing in these three measured split summaries.
- Severe imbalance and right censoring require suitable modeling and evaluation when we reach the predictive modeling phase.
- This dataset is **real SCANIA truck-fleet data**, not direct data from a manufacturing production line.
- SCANIA, Mendeley AI4I and Kaggle images have no established shared machine identifiers and **must not be treated as synchronized multimodal measurements**.
